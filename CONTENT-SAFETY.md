# Content and safety notes

This project deliberately separates:
1. canonical scripture text,
2. sourced translations,
3. AI-generated Hindi meaning,
4. AI-generated relevance explanation.

Do not let the AI generate Sanskrit scripture.

For users expressing imminent self-harm or suicide intent, the website should prioritize immediate human support rather than presenting scripture as a substitute for crisis care.

India's Ministry of Health and Family Welfare currently lists Tele-MANAS as a 24x7 tele-mental-health service and gives 14416 / 1800-89-14416 as access numbers.
