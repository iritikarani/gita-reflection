The Bhagavad Gita on failure

After a failure, it's easy to let one result speak for our whole worth. The Gita gently separates the two: our effort is ours, but the outcome depends on many things beyond us.

Some of the Gita's most tender lines are addressed to someone who tried and fell short.

How the Gita approaches this

The Gita's teaching on action (karma yoga) asks us to act sincerely and remain even-minded in success and failure. It also reassures that no sincere effort is ever wasted, and that every undertaking carries some imperfection — 'as fire is clouded by smoke'.

Verses to reflect on

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 6, Verse 40

पार्थ नैवेह नामुत्र विनाशस्तस्य विद्यते।
न हि कल्याणकृत्कश्चिद्दुर्गतिं तात गच्छति॥

pārtha naiveha nāmutra vināśas tasya vidyate
na hi kalyāṇa-kṛt kaścid durgatiṁ tāta gacchati

Translation

Arjuna, there is no loss for such a person, either in this world or the next. One who does good, my dear friend, never comes to a bad end.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

हे पार्थ, ऐसे व्यक्ति का न इस लोक में नाश होता है, न परलोक में। हे तात, कल्याणकारी कर्म करने वाला कभी दुर्गति को प्राप्त नहीं होता।

In simple words Interpretation

Sincere effort is never lost — even when you fall short. Krishna calls Arjuna 'tāta', a tender word, like 'my dear'.

When this may help: When you feel you've failed despite genuinely trying.

A question to sit with: What good did your effort create, even if the result wasn't what you hoped?

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 2, Verse 48

योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनञ्जय।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते॥

yoga-sthaḥ kuru karmāṇi saṅgaṁ tyaktvā dhanañjaya
siddhy-asiddhyoḥ samo bhūtvā samatvaṁ yoga ucyate

Translation

Established in yoga, perform your actions, Arjuna, letting go of attachment. Remain even-minded in success and failure — this evenness of mind is called yoga.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

हे धनंजय, योग में स्थित होकर, आसक्ति त्यागकर कर्म करो। सफलता और असफलता में समान भाव रखो — यही समत्व योग कहलाता है।

In simple words Interpretation

Steadiness isn't about winning or losing. It's about staying yourself in both.

When this may help: When your mood rises and falls with every outcome, or one result seems to decide your worth.

A question to sit with: How would you treat yourself today if success and failure carried equal weight?

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 2, Verse 40

नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते।
स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात्॥

nehābhikrama-nāśo 'sti pratyavāyo na vidyate
sv-alpam apy asya dharmasya trāyate mahato bhayāt

Translation

On this path, no effort is ever lost and no harm is done. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

इस मार्ग में किया गया कोई भी प्रयास व्यर्थ नहीं जाता और न ही उसका कोई उल्टा परिणाम होता है। इसका थोड़ा-सा अभ्यास भी बड़े भय से रक्षा करता है।

In simple words Interpretation

No sincere effort is wasted. Even a small step counts.

When this may help: When you feel your efforts have been pointless, or you're afraid to start because you can't do it perfectly.

A question to sit with: What small step could you take today that would still count, even if no one saw it?

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 18, Verse 48

सहजं कर्म कौन्तेय सदोषमपि न त्यजेत्।
सर्वारम्भा हि दोषेण धूमेनाग्निरिवावृताः॥

saha-jaṁ karma kaunteya sa-doṣam api na tyajet
sarvārambhā hi doṣeṇa dhūmenāgnir ivāvṛtāḥ

Translation

Do not abandon the work that comes naturally to you, Arjuna, even if it has flaws. For every undertaking is clouded by some imperfection, as fire is by smoke.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

हे कौन्तेय, स्वाभाविक कर्म को दोषयुक्त होने पर भी नहीं छोड़ना चाहिए, क्योंकि जैसे धुएँ से अग्नि ढकी रहती है, वैसे ही सभी कर्म किसी न किसी दोष से युक्त होते हैं।

In simple words Interpretation

Nothing worth doing is flawless. Every fire has some smoke — keep going anyway.

When this may help: When perfectionism is stopping you, or a mistake makes you want to give up.

A question to sit with: Where has the fear of imperfection been stopping you?

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 4, Verse 36

अपि चेदसि पापेभ्यः सर्वेभ्यः पापकृत्तमः।
सर्वं ज्ञानप्लवेनैव वृजिनं सन्तरिष्यसि॥

api ced asi pāpebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ pāpa-kṛt-tamaḥ
sarvaṁ jñāna-plavenaiva vṛjinaṁ santariṣyasi

Translation

Even if you were the greatest of all wrongdoers, you would cross over every wrong by the boat of knowledge alone.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

यदि तुम सब पापियों से भी अधिक पाप करने वाले हो, तब भी ज्ञान रूपी नौका द्वारा तुम सब पापों को पार कर जाओगे।

In simple words Interpretation

No mistake puts you beyond the reach of understanding and a fresh start.

When this may help: When guilt or regret makes you feel you can't begin again.

A question to sit with: What would you say to a friend carrying the regret you're carrying?
