The Bhagavad Gita on fear

The whole Gita is spoken on a battlefield, to someone afraid of what is about to happen. Arjuna's fear is not mocked; it is met with patience, friendship and a wider view.

The teachings that follow offer several ways to hold fear: by returning to what is in our control, by remembering what is lasting in us, and by taking small steps anyway.

How the Gita approaches this

Fear often lives in the future. The Gita repeatedly brings attention back to present action, reminds us that feelings come and go like seasons, and names fearlessness (abhayam) as the very first of the noble qualities.

Verses to reflect on

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 2, Verse 3

क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः पार्थ नैतत्त्वय्युपपद्यते।
क्षुद्रं हृदयदौर्बल्यं त्यक्त्वोत्तिष्ठ परन्तप॥

klaibyaṁ mā sma gamaḥ pārtha naitat tvayy upapadyate
kṣudraṁ hṛdaya-daurbalyaṁ tyaktvottiṣṭha paran-tapa

Translation

Do not yield to this weakness, Arjuna; it does not suit you. Cast off this small faintness of heart and rise, O scorcher of foes.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

हे पार्थ, इस कायरता को मत अपनाओ, यह तुम्हें शोभा नहीं देती। हृदय की इस तुच्छ दुर्बलता को त्यागकर उठ खड़े हो।

In simple words Interpretation

You are stronger than this moment is telling you. When you're ready, you can rise.

When this may help: When you need courage, or when self-doubt is telling you a smaller story about yourself than is true.

A question to sit with: What would you do today if you trusted your own strength a little more?

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 2, Verse 40

नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते।
स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात्॥

nehābhikrama-nāśo 'sti pratyavāyo na vidyate
sv-alpam apy asya dharmasya trāyate mahato bhayāt

Translation

On this path, no effort is ever lost and no harm is done. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

इस मार्ग में किया गया कोई भी प्रयास व्यर्थ नहीं जाता और न ही उसका कोई उल्टा परिणाम होता है। इसका थोड़ा-सा अभ्यास भी बड़े भय से रक्षा करता है।

In simple words Interpretation

No sincere effort is wasted. Even a small step counts.

When this may help: When you feel your efforts have been pointless, or you're afraid to start because you can't do it perfectly.

A question to sit with: What small step could you take today that would still count, even if no one saw it?

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 16, Verse 1

अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः।
दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम्॥

abhayaṁ sattva-saṁśuddhir jñāna-yoga-vyavasthitiḥ
dānaṁ damaś ca yajñaś ca svādhyāyas tapa ārjavam

Translation

Fearlessness, purity of heart, steadfastness in knowledge and practice, generosity, self-restraint, sacrifice, study, discipline and straightforwardness —

हिन्दी भावार्थ

निर्भयता, अंतःकरण की शुद्धि, ज्ञान और योग में दृढ़ स्थिति, दान, इंद्रियों का संयम, यज्ञ, स्वाध्याय, तप और सरलता —

In simple words Interpretation

The first quality the Gita lists in a noble nature is fearlessness. Courage is the ground other virtues grow from.

When this may help: When you need courage to be honest, generous or true to yourself.

A question to sit with: What would you do differently if you were just a little less afraid?

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 9, Verse 22

अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते।
तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम्॥

ananyāś cintayanto māṁ ye janāḥ paryupāsate
teṣāṁ nityābhiyuktānāṁ yoga-kṣemaṁ vahāmy aham

Translation

To those who remember me with undivided attention and are always devoted, I bring what they lack and preserve what they have.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

जो लोग अनन्य भाव से मेरा चिंतन करते हुए मेरी उपासना करते हैं, उन नित्य जुड़े हुए भक्तों का योगक्षेम (जो नहीं है उसकी प्राप्ति और जो है उसकी रक्षा) मैं स्वयं वहन करता हूँ।

In simple words Interpretation

You don't have to carry everything alone. In the Gita's vision, those who turn toward the Divine are cared for.

When this may help: When worry about the future feels heavy and you long to trust that you'll be looked after.

A question to sit with: What would you set down today if you trusted you'd be cared for?

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 2, Verse 14

मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः।
आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत॥

mātrā-sparśās tu kaunteya śītoṣṇa-sukha-duḥkha-dāḥ
āgamāpāyino 'nityās tāṁs titikṣasva bhārata

Translation

Contact with the world brings cold and heat, pleasure and pain. These come and go; they are not permanent. Learn to bear them patiently, Arjuna.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

हे कौन्तेय, इंद्रियों और विषयों के संपर्क से सर्दी-गर्मी और सुख-दुख आते हैं। ये आते-जाते रहते हैं, स्थायी नहीं हैं। हे भारत, इन्हें धैर्य से सहन करो।

In simple words Interpretation

What you feel right now is real — and it is also passing. Like weather, it will change.

When this may help: When a feeling seems like it will last forever, or a hard season feels endless.

A question to sit with: What is one feeling you've carried before that eventually passed?

A simple practice

Write down what you are afraid will happen. Then write what is actually true right now, in this moment. Notice the difference.
