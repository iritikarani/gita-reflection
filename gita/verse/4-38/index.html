Bhagavad Gita 4.38

Clarity comes in its own time — and it comes from within.

Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 4, Verse 38

न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते।
तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति॥

na hi jñānena sadṛśaṁ pavitram iha vidyate
tat svayaṁ yoga-saṁsiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati

Translation

Nothing in this world purifies like knowledge. One who is mature in yoga finds it within oneself, in time.

हिन्दी भावार्थ

इस संसार में ज्ञान के समान पवित्र करने वाला कुछ भी नहीं है। योग में सिद्ध व्यक्ति समय आने पर उसे स्वयं अपने भीतर पा लेता है।

In simple words Interpretation

Clarity comes in its own time — and it comes from within.

Context

Krishna describes wisdom as something that ripens with practice and patience, rather than something received all at once.

When this may help

When you're impatient for answers or frustrated that you haven't 'figured it out' yet.

Reflection question

What are you trying to understand faster than it can be understood?

A small practice

Leave one question unanswered today. Write it down and let it rest.
